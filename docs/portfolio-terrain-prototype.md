## Implementation

- `terrain-lettering.ts`: extracts original comic glyph outlines, constructs seven stepped strata per glyph with preserved holes, topographic outline loops, warm materials and directional light. Static relief, not animated typography. Successful-frame fallback switching; bounded DPR 1.5; resize/theme redraws only; explicit GPU resource disposal. Current relief costs 322 draw calls per static render; not advertised as optimized for every device.
- `PaperSurface.tsx`: one physically displaced sheet in the existing demand scene. Peripheral shallow hills, normal-based directional shading, fine grain and contour shader; feathered at top/bottom. No idle animation or additional surface canvas.
- `global.css` and original `public/art/portfolio/paper-surface.svg`: homepage-only warm paper material and embossed-looking peripheral contours, including no-JS/reduced/failure fallback. Dark palette is warm charcoal. SVG is locally authored deterministic vector rings and procedural SVG grain; no third-party artwork or network texture.
- `walking-route.ts`: feet-space A* routing with actor footprint clearance, exact segment/rectangle tests, bounded decorative wiggles, arc-length sampling, return corridor cost and measured route separation. Every completed segment is checked. A whole wiggle segment falls back to its validated straight segment, never an unchecked join. No clear/distinct route means native selection only, not clipping through the heading.
- `walking-view.ts`: one cancellable trip-only frame loop; stepping legs, opposite arm swing, body bob and facing changes; progressively revealed dots, max 220 per leg, removed afterward. Separate outbound/return traces; brief fade after return. Phones use a 32px-wide walking actor to fit the terrain margins. Long trips can take about 5.5 seconds including both legs/impact; this is marketing choreography, not a navigation delay.
- `SiteHeader.astro`: opaque paper foreground shelves keep name/navigation readable while the actor passes behind the phone navigation row. Hero heading/CTA remain true obstacles. `Avatar.ts` refreshes viewport clipping on reset, fixing stale-width overflow after cancellation on resize.
